## Hey, I'm levancho

I like figuring out how things work, wiring them together, and seeing what happens. Usually that means writing code, testing an idea, breaking something, then digging through the logs until it makes sense.

These days I'm tinkering with AI, automation and filmmaking tools. I like changing one thing, running it again, and finding out whether it actually got better. Sometimes an experiment turns into a useful tool. Sometimes it turns into another experiment.

**L3V** is where I put a lot of that work: tools I build, visual ideas I test, and the occasional trip down a cinema rabbit hole. The engineering is half the fun—getting something interesting out of it is the other half.

### Things you can poke around in

- **[My tools — tools.l3v.ai](https://tools.l3v.ai)** — the place to try the AI and creative tools I'm working on.
- **[AI Cinema Toolkit](https://toolkit.l3v.ai/)** — dig into lighting, lenses, camera movement, focus and what makes a shot work. I like understanding the knobs, not just turning them.
- **[AI video model guide](https://github.com/despanitech/AI-l3v-tools-UI)** — model strengths, limitations and practical workflows. Useful when you're trying to work out which tool fits the job.
- **[L3V](https://l3v.ai)** — the visual side of the experiments: worlds, characters and film ideas.
- **[Echora](https://www.youtube.com/@echoramedia)** — where the films end up. Have a look at what came out of all that testing.

I also build software and practical AI tools through **[Despani Technologies](https://despanitech.com)**.
